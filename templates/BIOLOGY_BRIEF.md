# Two-page biology brief — content specification

**Template only. No scientific findings are populated.** Audience: a biological research team assessing whether a candidate mechanism deserves further work.

## Page 1 — what the data support

1. **Decision in one sentence:** describe which candidates, if any, merit follow-up and the principal reason.
2. **Question and data:** identify tissue, phase, assay and actual independent donor counts after exclusions; distinguish original atlas totals from analyzed subjects.
3. **Main evidence:** one donor-flow figure and one discovery/replication effect figure, with missingness and uncertainty visible.
4. **Method comparison:** explain whether graph propagation added useful cross-study signal over simple expression and degree controls. Report exact numerators/denominators; do not translate concordance into target accuracy.

## Page 2 — recommendation and limits

1. **Candidate evidence table:** up to five protocol-selected dossiers; separate expression transfer, genetic evidence and intervention-direction uncertainty.
2. **Counterevidence:** include conflicting/inconclusive candidates and the main alternate explanations.
3. **Remaining uncertainty:** cell versus nucleus measurement, phase/control differences, small donor counts and missing functional evidence.
4. **Research recommendation:** follow up, hold or deprioritize with reasons; name what public evidence could change the decision.
5. **Reproducibility:** link frozen input/config versions, analysis release and actual cloud receipt. If a run was not completed, mark it unexecuted.

A completed negative-result report is acceptable. Do not substitute hypothetical genes or figures for unavailable results. A Japanese executive summary can be added after the scientific English report is stable.
