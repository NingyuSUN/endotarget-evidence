# Candidate research-target dossier — template

**Unfilled template. No gene has been selected or evaluated.**

Use one dossier per protocol-selected candidate, including conflicting/inconclusive examples. Replace prompts with actual sourced findings; never fill unknowns with favorable assumptions.

## Research recommendation

Gene ID/symbol; dossier selection category; follow up / hold / deprioritize; a short reason; date and analysis release.

## Expression evidence

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Biological contrast | Tissue, lineage, phase, hormone restriction and control definition |
| Discovery evidence | Actual case/control donors and cells; effect, interval, model and covariates |
| Replication evidence | Actual donors/nuclei; effect, interval, coverage and fit status |
| Rank provenance | B0/G1 ranks, frozen universe/config hashes and shortlist membership |
| Robustness | Donor sensitivity, cell-state composition, age/batch and assay limitations |

State whether the external result is concordant, conflicting, inconclusive or unavailable. Lack of statistical significance alone is not a contradiction.

## Genetic and other public evidence

For each item: underlying study, URL, version, ancestry/tissue, association locus, gene-mapping method, direction where supported, and whether it reuses the expression sources. Separate positional links from stronger gene-mapping evidence. Do not call multiple database mirrors independent support.

## Interpretation and counterevidence

Explain the biological hypothesis in plain English. Address alternative causes of the expression signal, control comorbidity, menstrual-stage variation and cell/nucleus differences. State whether the hypothesized effect is relevant to this tissue and whether intervention direction is actually known.

## What would change the recommendation?

Name a specific disconfirming observation or needed public evidence. If none is publicly available, say so. New wet experiments or private data are not required project tasks; the missing evidence remains a limit of the recommendation.

## Claim boundary

This dossier supports a research-prioritization judgment. It does not establish functional target validation, an effective drug, clinical utility or an instruction to treat patients.

## Traceability

Link exact frozen analysis tables, source evidence, software/config versions and reviewer comments. Record human decisions and AI assistance separately.
